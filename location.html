Location & Accommodation

Nyungwe National Park is located in southwestern Rwanda. It is about 225 km from Kigali and can be reached by road through the Kigali-Huye-Rusizi or Kigali-Karongi-Rusizi routes.

📍 Where Is Nyungwe?

Nyungwe is in southwestern Rwanda, close to the borders with Burundi and the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

🚗 How to Get There

From Kigali, visitors can travel by road to Nyungwe. The journey is approximately 225 km and normally takes several hours depending on the route and road conditions.

Another option is to fly from Kigali International Airport to Kamembe Airport and continue by road to Nyungwe.

✈️ Nearest Airport

Kamembe Airport is the nearest airport to Nyungwe. From Kamembe, visitors can continue by road toward the park's Gisakura area.
