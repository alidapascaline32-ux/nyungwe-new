About Nyungwe Forest

Nyungwe Forest is one of Rwanda's most remarkable natural treasures. It is a beautiful rainforest known for its rich biodiversity, fascinating wildlife and spectacular scenery.


The forest provides visitors with opportunities to explore nature, discover wildlife, enjoy hiking trails and experience the famous canopy walkway.




Why Nyungwe Is Special

Nyungwe is important because it provides a home for many species of plants, birds and animals. It also plays an important role in protecting Rwanda's natural environment.
