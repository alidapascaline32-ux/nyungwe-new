Explore Nyungwe's Attractions

Nyungwe Forest offers visitors a wonderful combination of wildlife, adventure, nature and unforgettable experiences.

Canopy Walkway

The Nyungwe Canopy Walkway offers visitors an exciting experience above the forest. Visitors can enjoy spectacular views of the rainforest and surrounding landscape.

Chimpanzee Tracking

Chimpanzee tracking gives visitors an opportunity to explore the forest and learn about one of its fascinating primate species.

Forest Hiking

Nyungwe has beautiful forest trails where visitors can walk through the rainforest, enjoy peaceful surroundings and discover incredible natural scenery.

Bird Watching

Nyungwe is an exciting destination for bird lovers. Visitors can explore the forest and observe a variety of fascinating bird species.
